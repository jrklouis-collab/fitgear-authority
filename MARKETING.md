# FitGear Authority — four-week promotion kit

Ready-to-use drafts. No social posts have been published. Use your own footage or images you have permission to use. Send people to the guides; Amazon links and disclosures are on those pages. Captions below include a plain affiliate disclosure too.

## Weekly routine (about 45 minutes)

- Monday: publish one useful comparison or label-reading tip from the drafts below.
- Wednesday: publish one short demonstration of the calculator or checklist.
- Friday: answer a real shopping question and link the relevant guide.
- Once weekly: record Search Console impressions/clicks and Amazon clicks/ordered items/shipped earnings in the scorecard. Update content based on actual queries; do not invent traffic.

Use a direct clickable website link in your Instagram bio. Caption URLs may not be clickable. For Stories, use a link sticker if available. No paid ads are needed for this starting routine.
